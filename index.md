## About Me

I'm a 5th year Ph.D. Candidate in Chemical Engineering at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, advised by [Dr. Joel Paulson](https://cbe.osu.edu/people/paulson.82). I develop sample-efficient methods for optimizing expensive black-box systems, spanning high-dimensional Bayesian optimization, Bayesian novelty search, and global optimization of Gaussian process models, with applications in material discovery, energy building system, and process system engineering.

Previously, I was a research intern at Dow Chemical (2025) and received my B.S. and M.S. in Chemical Engineering from National Taiwan University, advised by [Dr. Jeffrey Ward](https://che.ntu.edu.tw/che/en/jeffward.html).

## Research Interests

- Bayesian optimization and Gaussian processes
- Deterministic global optimization and nonlinear programming
- Multi-armed bandits and decision-making under uncertainty
- Process systems engineering
