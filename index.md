## About Me

I'm a 5th year Ph.D. Candidate in Chemical Engineering at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, advised by [Dr. Joel Paulson](https://cbe.osu.edu/people/paulson.82).

I received my B.S. and M.S. in Chemical Engineering at National Taiwan University, where I was advised by [Dr. Jeffrey Ward](https://che.ntu.edu.tw/che/en/jeffward.html).

## Research Interests

- Bayesian optimization and Gaussian processes
- Decision-making under uncertainty
- Deterministic global optimization
- Process systems engineering
